# PlanetPulse — Decision Points

## DP1 — The Nudge

We chose to encourage users instead of shaming or blocking them. At 80% of the weekly target, PlanetPulse shows small challenges that give users a practical way to reduce their footprint.

Once the user reaches 100% of the target, the challenges are replaced with sustainability tips and useful resources. This keeps the app helpful even when the target has already been exceeded.

## DP2 — Absurd Input

We decided to reject obviously unrealistic quantities using predefined limits. For example, a car entry above 10,000 km is not accepted.

This prevents a simple input mistake from massively distorting the user's weekly footprint and progress.

## DP3 — The Week

We chose Monday to Sunday as the weekly tracking period. The current week's activities are used for the dashboard and target progress, while the history and weekly trend let users look at activity across different dates.

Using a fixed Monday-Sunday period makes the weekly target easier to understand and compare.
